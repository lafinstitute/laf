The guide identifies Tomasz Michałek-Czerepak and Tomasz Świerszcz as authors and Dr. hab. Andrzej Sobczak, prof. SGH as collaborator. The source states that it is offered under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

The source PDF is not copied into this repository. Page/section references below refer to the supplied eight-page PDF (printed guide pages 2–21; PDF page numbering may differ). Summaries and learning materials in this repository are derivative explanatory materials and should retain appropriate attribution and license notices when distributed.

## Scope and provenance

The knowledge base summarizes LAF Guide v1.6, especially its glossary and sections 1–5.2. Content explicitly labeled **LAF 1.6** is a paraphrase of the guide. Content labeled **Objaśnienie**, **Przykład**, or **Ćwiczenie** is added to aid understanding and is not asserted to be a normative LAF rule. Where the source leaves a choice to an organization, the material says so.

## Version caveat

This repository describes version 1.6 only. It does not claim that v1.6 is the latest edition. Confirm the applicable guide version with the LAF Institute or course provider before relying on it for a course or organizational decision.
